# Phase 8 — Machine Diagnostics & Condition-Monitoring Scope (2026-07-19)

> **Status: SOFTWARE PLATFORM IMPLEMENTED; PHYSICAL DETECTORS PARTIAL (reconciled 2026-07-24).** In addition to Phases 1+2, the current working trees implement persistent timing baselines, mid-session stuck/beam-blocked rules, the IN-B manual-intervention path, bounded durable diagnostic transport/outboxes, exact paired-node identity and health, clock/reset evidence, camera-capture FIFO/receipts, diagnostic-delivery health, and crash-visible alert/backup safety ledgers. The remaining gaps are physical or calibration-dependent: §3.2 cam polarity/lobe capture; post-spot camera full-rack and gripper cross-check detectors; OS cavity tracing; BE/S/T current sensing; SC/TB attribution (not possible from the present chassis wiring); the physically open and uncharacterized J14.3–4 Stop/control-power interface; lane-21/22 pit-interlock disposition; demand-to-master/control-power-drop proof; protective-earth and supply-polarity commissioning; and first-article qualification. Statements such as “currently invisible” below describe the 2026-07-19 input inventory unless a later implementation note supersedes them. The current closure/proof boundary is recorded in `phase8_revD_round4_remediation_report_2026-07-23.md`.
> **Round-5 release-candidate amendment (2026-07-24; locally tested, not deployed or physically proven):** the lane daemon now fails startup unless **exactly one** of `WSL_CONTROLLER_SHADOW=1` or `WSL_LIVE_OUTPUTS=1` is selected; holds one lifetime cross-process controller-owner lease before state or hardware access; latches ARM prerequisites fail-safe and requires a fresh PBZ after recovery; authorizes identity only through an exact `WSL_RP2040_QUALIFIED_RELEASES=board|build|cfg` tuple (never independent-list cross-products); reserves and rechecks heartbeat freshness at the closest software-controlled positive-write boundary with bounded transport and rollback; and carries expanded identity/tap/platform truth in periodic machine-heartbeat processing. Focused and isolated repository tests pass. These changes still require clean committed-state review, Pi deployment, and bench/first-article validation.
>
> **Actuation-deadline boundary (2026-07-24):** the controller reserves an exact heartbeat-lease margin before every positive motor/ARM/watchdog operation, rechecks at the closest software-controlled physical-write boundary, bounds serial writes, measures the returned operation duration, and postchecks with rollback/hard-safe handling. This reduces software TOCTOU exposure but is **not a mathematical hard-real-time guarantee**: a Linux kernel, I²C ioctl, or GPIO call can begin while fresh and then stall opaquely. The independent `RP2040_OK` rail permission, NE555 watchdog, and Candidate-C OEM S/T guard remain load-bearing for that irreducible interval. The controller does not pre-emptively de-arm while staging a positive motion command, because doing so could interrupt an already-running mechanical channel.
>
> **Control-loop continuity boundary (2026-07-24):** before any tick can dispatch an event, poll the FSM, assert ARM, energize a motor, or kick the watchdog, the daemon compares a dedicated monotonic clock with the last successfully completed tick's entry sample, then repeats that continuity sample after acquiring the link transaction. The post-lock sample prevents a delayed lock acquisition plus a newly arrived heartbeat from permitting one resumed positive tick. A gap over 1.0 s (well below the bench-measured ~10 s NE555 expiry), clock regression, or invalid clock sample latches a hard-safe episode, emits a durable fault-severity `rail_drop/controller_loop_gap` diagnostic even if ARM was already low, and requires a post-trip PBZ release/re-press. A fresh heartbeat received by the independent serial reader cannot erase this latch. Linux uses `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` when Python exposes it, so suspend time is included; the fallback `time.monotonic()` may exclude system suspend on some platforms. Neither clock can pre-empt an opaque kernel/I²C/GPIO stall after the final software check, so the physical NE555, `RP2040_OK`, and OEM guard remain load-bearing. This amendment is locally tested, not deployed or bench-proven.
> **External sensor-supply amendment (2026-07-24; software implemented, harness not installed):** Rev-D **AUX10** is reserved for `sensor_24v_ok`, an external voltage monitor's isolated, healthy-when-closed dry contact. `sensor_supply_lost` / `sensor_supply_restored` are contract event types. While that contact is lost, missing, or not yet observed, the dependent exit-photoeye and distributor-index signals are UNKNOWN. For those two roles, UNKNOWN immediately discards pending return timers, distributor-cycle anchors, and pulse-stale counters because the missing event may have happened unseen. IN-B bank UNKNOWN or FIELD_WET blindness additionally invalidates BE-current windows and field-input held-time anchors; external sensor-24V loss alone does **not** suppress a separately powered BE-current sensor. Recovery establishes only a level baseline; the pair exit tracker then drains one full configured return timeout, and only wholly post-recovery balls/cycles start new claims. The rule is alert-only and dormant until explicitly mapped. **Never connect 24 V directly to AUX10.** Installation remains NO-GO until AUX10 passes Rev-D first-article qualification and the contact/harness passes the open-wire, supply-loss, restoration, and no-immediate-dependent-alert gate in `phase8_revD_first_article_pack.md` FA-5.
> **Stop/master-breaker correction (2026-07-24; P0 physical gate):** the board provides a series source position at J14.3–4, but the authoritative harness build sheet and Section-F spec say those conductors are physically **OPEN**, have no measured dry tap, and must never be jumpered at the machine. Therefore the rail cannot arm in a field installation today, and no current diagnostic can prove that a Stop demand removes machine power. A later physical inspection found **no C.I.S. device or wiring on pilot lanes 21/22**; their installed chain is Stop-only, and whether another pit-entry interlock exists remains open. A valid pilot architecture needs a separately attributable `stop_request` plus an independent isolated downstream `control_power_ok`/breaker-auxiliary proof and bounded Stop→power-drop functional test. If an existing/new pit interlock becomes part of the approved design, observe and demand-test it separately; a switch placed only in J14 is board permission, not an upstream final disconnect and cannot stop a welded contact. A static “healthy” contact cannot reveal a bypass until demanded. These candidate signals may use Rev-D GPB dry-contact capacity only through listed galvanically isolated monitor contacts after their electrical form and landing are measured; no machine mains or unclassified live ladder may enter J15 or any SAFE_* net.
> **Availability and provisioning rule:** IN-B bank UNKNOWN, FIELD_WET loss, and external sensor-24V loss form one union-of-unavailability episode for exit/index diagnostics. Overlapping outages invalidate once and remain blind until every configured prerequisite is healthy; no absence anchor is shifted or resumed. Cycle-completion alarms are resolved only after the current tick's diagnostic sample, and IN-B debounce state is discarded across bank/channel UNKNOWN so recovery cannot synthesize an edge. Runtime diagnostic kill-switch intervals use the same invalidation-and-recovery-drain semantics. A two-board service must use explicit maps such as `WSL_DIAG_AUX_ROLES_L21=...` and `WSL_DIAG_AUX_ROLES_L22=...`; once either board is explicitly mapped, omission of its mate is a startup error and an exact blank value is required to declare an intentionally unmapped board. The unscoped `WSL_DIAG_AUX_ROLES` compatibility setting is one-board-only, and startup refuses more than one `exit_beam` mapping for the pair.
>
> **Diagnostic-delivery integrity amendment (2026-07-24; locally verified, not deployed):** the runtime master gate, a failed board, a full writer queue, a server per-record reject, and process shutdown no longer consume one-shot evidence silently. Current conditions are canceled and re-proven after blind intervals; immutable incidents retain their original UTC/monotonic occurrence stamp. Bounded retry sets are severity-first (fault, then warning, then information), coalesce repeated identical incidents without overwriting first/last evidence, and interleave final offer/drain passes even when the writer queue has capacity one. Platform clock steps and service-start/restart-loop records likewise retry immutable occurrences; controller start obligations share the fsync'd counter state and are acknowledged only after a local JSONL persistence receipt. Cross-service relays use an atomically fsync'd write-ahead ledger: the planner transition and every concrete lane obligation must fit before any writer offer, each row is pre-stamped with a restart-stable `source_id/boot_id/seq`, and an ambiguous durable-write failure is replayed with that exact identity. A later healthy sample never cancels such an ambiguous alert; the alert is completed idempotently before its exact-family recovery. Server resolution requires a later informational `recovered` event with an exact target/family and producer chronology newer than the newest unresolved recurrence. `outbox_quarantine` rejects are quarantined but cannot recursively generate another notifier. A writer join timeout never starts a competing synchronous sink drain. Live platform health is derived from current probe state rather than delivery latches, so a rejected event cannot make a heartbeat green. These rules improve forensic honesty; they do not create new control authority.

**Cross-cutting: SC+TB and cam polarity (powered ground truth beats cold continuity and the manuals).** The replacement harness exposes only one non-isolatable live-ladder region at C2A-U and no dry TB contact or valid J_SAFE landing; GP11/J3-6 therefore has no independent TB signal on lanes 21/22 (`04_machine-io.md:41-48`). The powered 2026-07-07 test established the actual OEM behavior: **parallel closed-when-safe contacts** permit a coil when either lever is pressed, and block both S and T coils when both levers are BACK/open. Candidate C is the decided **primary hardware guard**: the controlled J_SAFE1-2 jumper delegates TB/SC protection to that OEM ladder, and every lane must pass the live G3 S-and-T coil-drop insertion-point proof. Consequences: (a) per-cam interlock attribution is impossible from the present harness; (b) the default-off firmware `interlock_collision` echo is an unvalidated secondary diagnostic, never the primary guard; (c) any echo remains blocked on bench-confirming its observable danger-window semantics; and (d) the other cam edge polarities/lobes remain calibration inputs until §3.2 is completed. Cold ~21 Ω sneak paths must not be promoted back into topology claims.

## 2. Three-layer architecture mapped to real components

### Layer 1 — Deterministic rules (hard faults, act-now)
**Hosts (existing):**
- **RP2040 firmware** (source v1.1.1; **which image is flashed on the machine-22 board is unverified** — resolve at the powered session): motion_timeout + chatter active; cam_overrun / motion_no_run / interlock_collision implemented but compiled OFF. Emits typed `{"ev":"flt","code":...}` JSON + drops RP_OK (`main.c:404-446`).
- **cycle_control_8270 FSM**: MAX_MOTION_S, GUARD_DELAY_MAX_S, two-press PBZ recovery (`cycle_control_8270.py:159-214,400-422`). ⚠️ The "refused-energize → FAULT" and "mid-motion interlock veto" paths (`:175,194-205,404-410`) are not validated collision protection on lanes 21/22 because the harness has no independent TB observation. Do not count the default-off echo as live detection capacity; Candidate C’s OEM ladder + per-lane G3 proof is primary.
- **controller_daemon**: startup stuck-input detection (`:260-279`), link health → `_on_safety_trip` — the single choke point every fault path already funnels through (`controller_daemon.py:306-330,391-408`) = the one place to attach an emitter.
- **rp2040_link**: fw_reboot detection, `run_mismatch()` (an unconsumed signal), TX-drop warnings (`rp2040_link.py:313-457`).

**New code needed:** structured fault codes — `_fault(why)` is free-text that never reaches the recorder (`cycle_control_8270.py:209-214`); else-branch logging/counting of unexpected cam edges; per-MOTOR energized-time (H-02); mid-session stuck-input + beam-blocked rules; IN_B read path; platform_health emitters. All must obey the established pattern: observe-only, bounded, **never raises into the 50 Hz tick that kicks the NE555** (`controller_daemon.py:26-30,286-292` — blocking work in any tick stalls BOTH lanes' kicks).

### Layer 2 — Fault-signature library (typed events + forensics)
**Hosts (existing):** FlightRecorder (2000-event ring, blackbox JSON on FAULT/link-loss/tick-error, 50-file local retention — `flight_recorder.py:47-55,107-118,300-316`); trace_replay for deterministic off-hardware FSM replay (`trace_replay.py:14-46`) — but **no blackbox→trace converter exists** (formats differ), so "replay a real fault in CI" needs that converter.
**Signature source:** the AMF troubleshooting content is **already extracted** — full TABLE TROUBLES / sweep / ball-lift cause-remedy tables (jam, Klixon-overload) sit in `Downloads/svc_text.txt` (+ `8270_text.txt`), referenced by `phase8_8270_SYSTEM_REFERENCE.md:60`. The missing pass is **distillation into a repo signature table**, not PDF mining.
**New code:** signature matcher over machine_events sequences (state, duration, input pattern → probable cause + mechanic hint), plus dump shipping off-Pi.

### Layer 3 — Fleet anomaly + trend detection
**Hosts (existing):** CamTelemetry (baselines + drift alarms, already in the tick loop). **Transport:** the Pi already runs a proven production websocket to the :8766 server's WS port (`lane_node.py:19,42`) — the work is the deliberately-deferred TODO(server) unification bridging the synchronous daemon into that async path (or an interim local HTTP POST), an *integration* task, not a new transport. **:8766 server:** `/api/health` is already a rich self-diagnostic surface (uptime, per-lane scoring summaries, save-persistence health — `lane_node_server.py:759-806`) — extend it, don't invent a parallel one. WS ingest currently accepts only HELLO/BALL_EVENT/FOUL_EVENT/HEARTBEAT (`lane_node_server.py:256-266`); add TELEMETRY/FAULT types. **wsl_api** pulls via the test-pinned bridge (`wsl_phase8_bridge.py:170-226`; contract test `tests/test_phase8_bridge_contract.py` — lockstep cross-repo change). **Analytics :5002** = read-only trend reporting per the roadmap rule (owns no schema — `RMS_ARCHITECTURE_ROADMAP_2026-07-10.md:116-120`).

### Data schema — new Machine/Equipment domain
The roadmap's 10 domains contain no Machine domain → this is a **new domain**: own tables, own vocabulary, never sharing rows or status enums with Fulfillment/Reporting. **Single owner: the lane-node server (:8766, its SQLite store).** wsl_api is a **pure proxy** — no second writable copy in wsl.db (a periodic *read-model* sync is allowed only if explicitly one-way; never a second writer). Desk ack/resolve is a **wsl_api-initiated POST to :8766** (the bridge stays strictly wsl_api-initiated; no inbound push). `acknowledged_by` stores the wsl.db staff id as an opaque reference across the domain boundary.

Timestamps UTC; explicit `business_date` (roadmap tier 3; precedent `tip_runs`); durations integer ms.

```sql
CREATE TABLE machine_cycles (
  id            INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
  lane_id       INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK(lane_id BETWEEN 1 AND 32),
  business_date TEXT NOT NULL,
  started_at    TEXT NOT NULL,               -- UTC ISO
  ended_at      TEXT,
  cycle_type    TEXT,                        -- writer-validated; NO CHECK (taxonomy will grow)
  ball          INTEGER,
  final_state   TEXT NOT NULL,               -- READY | FAULT | MANUAL_INTERVENTION
  aborted       INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  -- the six CamTelemetry intervals, integer ms, NULL when not observed:
  ss_to_guard_ms INTEGER, guard_to_table_ms INTEGER, table_to_ta2_ms INTEGER,
  ta2_to_sa_ms  INTEGER, sa_to_ta1zero_ms INTEGER, bs_to_ta1zero_ms INTEGER,
  gs_mask       INTEGER,                     -- 10-bit gripper mask latched at TA2
  cam_mask      INTEGER,                     -- 10-bit camera standing-pin mask (when available)
  fw_version    TEXT, shadow INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
);
CREATE INDEX idx_mc_lane_date ON machine_cycles(lane_id, business_date);

CREATE TABLE machine_events (
  id            INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
  lane_id       INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK(lane_id BETWEEN 1 AND 32),
  business_date TEXT NOT NULL,
  created_at    TEXT NOT NULL,               -- UTC
  severity      TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(severity IN ('info','warn','fault')),  -- the ONLY CHECK enum
  event_type    TEXT NOT NULL,               -- writer-validated, NO CHECK: fsm_fault, fw_fault,
                                             -- drift_alarm, chatter, link_lost, fw_reboot,
                                             -- rp2040_wdt_reset, rail_drop, pi_undervoltage,
                                             -- service_restart, stuck_input, beam_blocked,
                                             -- gripper_disagree, short_rack, run_mismatch,
                                             -- manual_override, recovered, ...
  code          TEXT,                        -- typed, e.g. 'motion_timeout:S'
  cycle_id      INTEGER REFERENCES machine_cycles(id),
  detail_json   TEXT,
  blackbox_file TEXT,
  acknowledged_at TEXT, acknowledged_by INTEGER, resolved_at TEXT,
  incident_id   INTEGER                      -- optional link to manual lane_incidents; no shared enum
);
CREATE INDEX idx_me_open ON machine_events(acknowledged_at, created_at);
```

Rationale for no CHECK on `event_type`/`cycle_type`: this project's own 2026-04-17 lesson — SQLite CHECK changes force a full table rebuild, and this taxonomy is guaranteed to grow (cam_overrun, motion_no_run, current-switch, camera cross-checks are all queued for later phases). `lane_incidents` (manual, `schema_analytics.sql:86-103`) stays as-is; machine_events may link via `incident_id` but never shares its vocabulary. Note the existing `lane_incidents` write endpoints live on password-gated :5002 and are dormant (zero UI callers) — machine ingestion inverts that: writes land on the operational side.

## 3. Alerting path

1. **Pi, off-tick:** attach an emitter at `_on_safety_trip` + CamTelemetry `cam_sink` + drift-alarm hook. Events → bounded in-memory queue; a writer thread (dump_async pattern, `flight_recorder.py:173-195`) ships them — **never from the tick thread**.
2. **Pi → server:** bridge into the existing lane_node.py websocket (the TODO(server) unification) or interim HTTP POST to :8766. New WS message types beyond the current 4.
3. **:8766 server (owner):** persists machine_cycles/machine_events, exposes `GET /api/lane/<N>/diagnostics`, enriches `/api/health`. Accepts the ack/resolve POST from wsl_api.
4. **wsl_api (:5000):** pure proxy via the existing bridge pattern (`_proxy_phase8_get`); machine fault state rides the **bulk `/api/lanes` payload** — mandatory, because the per-lane scoring fanout polls OPEN lanes only (`desk.html:6157-6159`) and idle-machine faults would otherwise be invisible. Contract test updated in lockstep.
5. **Desk (≤5 s via existing poll, `desk.html:6224-6228`; polling is the house pattern — no WS/SSE anywhere):** extend the shipped `__scoringOffline` trouble primitive (`desk.html:6173-6180`) with fault badges ("TABLE STALLED", "PIN JAM?"); toast on new fault (`.dv-toast`); persistent banner for unresolved faults (CONNECTION LOST pattern); ack/resolve queue copied from shoe_requests. **Desk UI is Design's deliverable via a `DESIGN_HANDOFF_machine_health.md`** (endpoints/shapes/acceptance) per the standing front-end split — backend + handoff doc are in-scope here, the UI build is not.
6. **Mechanic SMS:** `from wsl_customer_app import _send_sms` — module-level, provider-agnostic, zero auth coupling (`wsl_customer_app.py:95-118`). Run off-thread (synchronous 10 s Twilio timeout); throttle + dedupe (one text per latched fault, not per poll). **Recipient resolution is NOT solvable from existing data**: `labor_schedule` is a DOW×hour×role×headcount *forecast template* with no staff identity — no roster, no time clock exists anywhere. v1 = an explicit **on-call contact** (config row, or `staff.role='mechanic'` + designated-contact flag + new `staff.phone` column — no staff phone exists today). A real shift roster is future work. **Verify TWILIO_* env on WSL-SRV at deploy** — machine-scoped env, and the Stripe incident proves deploys silently strip env vars; bake it into deploy verify.

**PUBLIC_PATHS — never expose:** `/api/machine/*`, `/api/lanes/*/diagnostics`, the :8766 server, ack endpoints, blackbox dump contents. Internal-LAN surfaces exactly like `/desk`/`/kds`. machine_events writes stay off :5002 (read-only per roadmap).

## 4. Hardware gaps and add-ons

**Actual spare capacity (verified against the netlist generator + generated .net):**
- **Immediately usable: exactly 3 input channels** — AUX1/AUX2/AUX3, J5 pins 9-11, IN-B GPA5-7, full PC817B front-ends, explicitly reserved to avoid a respin. "Usable" = hardware-complete; needs the IN_B_MAP software read path.
- **One vacant fast, edge-capable, isolated input:** TB/GP11 (J3 pin 6) — there is no independent TB harness signal on the 21/22 chassis → the only no-new-hardware candidate for a **once-per-rev encoder index pulse**. Any reuse must be coordinated with the pending secondary-echo decision. Full quadrature has **no compliant path** — all 8 fast inputs committed; PC817B bandwidth unspecified. AUX channels can take a low-rate index at ms-class MCP-interrupt latency.
- **Respin-only spares:** 8 IN-B GPB pins (no optos/connector), 5 OUT-A bits (no drivers), ~15 RP2040 GPIOs (unrouted), 0x23 OUT-B address (no footprint).
- **ADC: none.** Pico ADC pins connect to nothing. No rail monitor, no voltage sense. The 24VAC-sense front-end is a documented population *option* with unverified footprints.
- **Pi GPIO headroom: UNKNOWN** — the per-pair Pi budget is a DRAFT full of `# assign` placeholders (`phase8_channel_allocation.md:137-150`). A USB ADC dodges the question entirely.

**Current sensing (the BE/M blind spot):** motor current never crosses the PCB → sensors clamp at machine contactor wiring regardless of board revision. Options: (1) **digital-threshold hall/CT current switch** → dry contact into AUX1/AUX2 — gets "BE running yes/no" + "above overload threshold"; closes the BE-invisible gap and detects welded-contact stuck-running. Budget wetting against the TMA-0505S 1W rail (~2 mA/channel; rail floats ~14 V unloaded — rev-C item 5 bleed NOT implemented). (2) **External isolated ADC module** (USB preferred) for real amplitude/trend data. Priority: **BE motor first** (unguarded, invisible, drives the whole bin-fill fault class), then S/T (stall-vs-dead-cam disambiguation, brake wear trending).

**Rev-D decision:** do **not** spin the board for diagnostics alone — every pilot-critical diagnostic fits the existing ADC/AUX capacity or an external isolated module. Rev-D is still worth building as the combined safety, clearance, identity, serviceability, capacity, and diagnostics revision. Preserve the VCC_5V ADC, four isolated rail-*predicate* taps, eight AUX inputs, protected external I²C, test pads, and board/firmware identity provisions; AUX10 supply supervision requires a monitor/contact harness, not another PCB spin. The four taps observe watchdog/ARM/RP_OK causes; they do **not** measure the actual `RELAY_ENABLE_RAIL` outcome and must never be described as physical rail feedback.

**Residual monitoring gaps after this software pass:** BE/M/S/T motor-current, overload, and uncommanded-current evidence still needs external CT/Hall sensing; dead-gripper versus camera disagreement and short-rack detection need a commissioned full-rack camera mask; offspot tracing needs OS; cam order/polarity/lobes require powered capture; SC/TB attribution is impossible from the present shared chassis wiring; the actual `RELAY_ENABLE_RAIL` outcome is not continuously sensed (so Q14/J14/output-rail failure is inferred, not directly observed); J14.3–4 has no landed Stop/control-power interface or demand-to-control-power-drop proof; lane 21/22 has no C.I.S. and the presence of another pit-entry interlock is unresolved; and protective-earth continuity plus hot/neutral polarity require a listed external instrument and electrician-controlled commissioning/periodic proof. A simple healthy contact cannot prove overvoltage or continuously reveal a welded bypass, and mechanical faults that do not perturb an observed signal remain invisible. PC817 hot/minimum-edge behavior, powered firmware identity/polarity, Cloudflare tunnel uniqueness, and off-disk restore are qualification/operations gates rather than more software detectors.

**Non-negotiables:** isolation contract §5.3 — no machine signal touches Pi/RP2040/MCP pins directly; LOGIC↔FIELD barrier only inside PC817s (≥2.5 mm), LOGIC↔MACHINE only inside G5LE relays (≥3.2 mm); never bridge GND/FIELD_GND. Five on-board rail conditions gate the pass-FET, while Candidate C’s OEM TB/SC ladder independently gates the S/T coils. Existing rail/status points are **observe-only**: never load, improvise jumpers on, or re-reference SAFE_*/RELAY_ENABLE_RAIL. J14.3–4 is physically open in the authoritative harness and must remain open until an approved Stop/control-power architecture, measured landing, and at-machine demand-to-power-drop proof exist; never jumper it merely to make the rail arm. The absent pilot C.I.S. cannot be replaced by a J14-only switch: any new final pit-entry interlock must act through an approved upstream safety-disconnect architecture. No mains conductor or protective-earth test circuit may enter Rev-D. The sole J_SAFE1-2 exception is the controlled Candidate-C harness jumper and its mandatory G3 coil-drop proof.

## 5. Phased build plan

Sequenced against real state: historic Rev-C bench/input-side evidence exists on **machine 22**; Rev-D R5 is geometry evidence only and remains unassembled/unqualified; Candidate C is decided from the powered OEM-ladder test but still requires per-lane G3 proof; ONE pilot machine remains the next physical step.

### Phase 0 — Powered-session ride-alongs (honest cost: several hours of probing, not zero; ranked so it's explicit what gets cut first)
Hard sequencing rule: **meter tapped-lead live voltages FIRST, board disconnected** (the standing session gate). Shadow-mode collection comes strictly *after* the metering→reconnect gate.
1. **§3.2 per-cam edge→angle polarity capture** (already a cutover-runbook item) — unblocks cam_overrun, motion_no_run, and every cam-sequence signature. Resolve TA1/SA lobe identities. *(Highest value — protect this one.)*
2. **Per-motion duration measurement** (stopwatch/scope over a few cycles) → seeds MAX_MOTION_S = measured+margin and initial baselines; today's only numbers are 12.1 RPM nominal, 3 s settle, and a "~8-10 s" code comment.
3. **Record which firmware image is flashed** (v0.1.0 vs v1.1.1 — behavior differs materially). Trivial, do it while connected.
4. **Cavity traces while metering anyway:** OS (promote it — it's the offspot detector), GP, PBC/10th/manual switches; cam front-end electrical class (dry vs 24 VAC).
5. **SC/TB secondary-echo characterization:** capture mechanical lever/window state against the OEM coil result without treating C2A-U as a dry landing. Candidate C’s primary proof is already defined: both levers BACK while S, then T, is commanded → both coils dead.
6. **After reconnect gate:** run `controller_daemon` in **SHADOW mode** with `WSL_CONTROLLER_SHADOW=1` and `WSL_LIVE_OUTPUTS=0`/unset — FSM on real inputs, outputs record-only, ARM held LOW — during any remaining powered time → first real cam-timing rows + blackbox-format data. The daemon now requires **exactly one** of `WSL_CONTROLLER_SHADOW=1` or `WSL_LIVE_OUTPUTS=1`; both/neither, approximate truthy spellings, and a bare default all refuse startup before hardware opens.

### Phase 1 — Software-only hardening on the Pi (source implemented; field qualification remains)
1. **Firmware-timestamp telemetry:** CamTelemetry consumes the cam event queue,
   uses a firmware-ms↔Pi-monotonic estimator, and invalidates clock-dependent
   state across reboot/discontinuity. Mixed-clock intervals still receive no
   false precision.
2. Structured fault codes, unexpected-edge counters, per-motor energized-time,
   mid-session stuck/beam-blocked rules, and the IN-B/manual-intervention path
   are implemented in source and isolated tests.
3. Platform health includes rail-drop/tap records, RP2040 watchdog/reset and
   identity evidence, Pi throttling/voltage status, service-start evidence, and
   bounded delivery health. Rev-D firmware is not yet flashed or physically
   qualified, so source support is not field evidence.
4. Cam baselines, blackbox evidence, and restart-critical diagnostic delivery
   obligations are persisted locally. Corrupt/ambiguous state fails visible;
   exact event identities survive retry and restart.

### Phase 2 — Transport + storage + surfacing (implemented in source; local release proof only)
The daemon→`:8766` durable bridge, single-owner machine store, diagnostic
endpoints, WSL proxy/bulk health state, desk health UI, mechanic-alert path,

## 6. Risks and honest limits

- **False-positive cost during league play.** A latched FAULT drops motors and needs two deliberate PBZ presses to recover — a false trip mid-league is a lane down and a mechanic walk. Therefore: Layer 2/3 detections are **alert-only, never actuate** — only the existing Layer-1 backstops keep trip authority. Drift alarms require 30 samples and persist across ordinary restart; any clock/reset/blind-interval discontinuity invalidates the affected baseline rather than fabricating continuity. Armed firmware detectors (`motion_no_run` has known nuisance paths: settle-back re-trip, MP manual buttons) soak in shadow/log-only first. Manual-override (IN-B) gates alert suppression.
- **Single-machine baseline limits.** Everything calibrates on machine 22 — also the *newest-maintained* machine, so its baselines will be optimistic. Signatures learned on 22 are priors, not thresholds, for the fleet.
- **Faults invisible to this input set (do not promise them):** BE/M overload/failure (Tier C only); welded contacts until motion_no_run is armed; dead gripper switches without the camera cross-check; offspot until OS is traced+consumed; per-cam interlock attribution (permanent on this chassis); anything mechanical that doesn't perturb cam timing, switch state, or the camera view. Open-circuit inputs read "condition absent" by design — absence-of-signal faults always need cross-evidence.
- **Safety non-negotiables.** No blocking I/O in the 50 Hz tick (a stall drops BOTH lanes' NE555 kicks); all diagnostics inherit observe-only/bounded/never-raise/async-write with env kill-switches; no new ARM or rail authority; never bridge GND/FIELD_GND; never weaken the watchdog — including "just this once" during debugging. NE555 timeout is bench-measured (~10 s), not source-declared.
- **Data volume.** Trivial by design (~low-thousands rows/day at 32 lanes), but: keep machine writes off the shared wsl.db hot path (wsl_api is a proxy, not a writer); blackbox dumps are 50 files/lane on Pi SD cards — batched local writes for SD wear, shipping + pruning matters at scale; raw 1 ms edge streams stay in the Pi ring buffer, only per-cycle aggregates + events go upstream.
- **Truth drift to watch:** the manual is generated from ordered
  `docs/manual_src` chapters and verified structurally, but installed wiring and
  flashed firmware remain higher-priority evidence than prose. The lane-21/22
  no-C.I.S. field finding supersedes OEM-generic Stop+C.I.S. descriptions. The
  flashed-firmware identity remains unknown until the bench record proves the
  exact build/config/board tuple; never infer it from source.
